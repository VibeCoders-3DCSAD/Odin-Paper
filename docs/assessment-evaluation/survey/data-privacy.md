## Data Privacy Notice

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), we are informing you how your survey responses will be collected, used, and protected.

Your participation is voluntary. Some questions are required for study analysis; if you do not wish to answer them, you may stop and choose not to submit the form. Optional questions, such as email address, may be left blank.

This survey collects information about your demographic profile, financial management practices, budgeting challenges, digital application use, and preferred features for the Buddi personal finance management system.

The optional email address field is collected only if you voluntarily provide it. If you leave the email address field blank, your response will be treated as anonymous. If you provide an email address, it will be used only for survey-related follow-up or clarification and will not be used to identify you in the study results.

All survey responses will be used only for academic research and for guiding the design, development, features, usability, and cultural relevance of Buddi. Results will be analyzed and reported in summary or aggregate form. Individual responses will not be disclosed to third parties.

Your data will be retained only for as long as necessary to complete this research study, including data analysis, validation, and reporting.
