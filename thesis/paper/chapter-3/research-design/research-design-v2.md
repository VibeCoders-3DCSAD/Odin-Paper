This study will utilize a combination of descriptive and developmental research design. The descriptive component will be used to examine the existing financial management practices of the intended users and to identify the problems they encounter in budgeting, saving, and managing debt. The developmental component will then take the findings of the examination as the basis for designing, developing, testing, and evaluating BUDGIE, a personal financial management app for Filipinos.

The descriptive component will be used to examine the financial management situation of Filipinos aged 18–59 in the National Capital Region, with the requirements of the intended users determined on the basis of the Public User Expectations and Perception Survey (PUEPS). The reference data for the proposed system will be established by temporally disaggregating the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Family Income and Expenditure Survey (FIES) 2023 using the Household Final Consumption Expenditure (HFCE) quarterly series covering 2022 Q1 to 2026 Q2, producing seasonally adjusted monthly expense estimates. These monthly expense estimates will be organized and analyzed using descriptive statistical techniques to present the spending characteristics and patterns of the intended users. The output of this phase is a set of user and system requirements. These requirements then serve as the basis for the design and development of the proposed application.

The developmental component will focus on the design, development, testing, and evaluation of BUDGIE based on the requirements identified during the descriptive phase. The monthly expense estimates incorporating seasonal expense patterns, established during the descriptive phase, will serve as inputs to the system’s analytical models. The development process will translate the identified requirements into system features built around the four models of the study: rule-based profile classification, SARIMA-based seasonal expense forecasting, IQR-based anomaly detection for unusual expenses, and linear programming for personalized budgets, per-goal savings contribution schedules, and per-debt repayment schedules under financial constraints. These models will be integrated into application features for expense recording, budgeting, savings and debt planning, seasonal spending forecasts, and unusual spending alerts, culminating in an actionable financial plan presented to the user for their approval. The completed system will undergo testing to verify functionality and reliability, followed by evaluation using structured instruments, including the System Usability Scale and measures aligned with the ISO/IEC 25010 software quality model. Testing and evaluation will be conducted against the requirements established in the descriptive phase.
