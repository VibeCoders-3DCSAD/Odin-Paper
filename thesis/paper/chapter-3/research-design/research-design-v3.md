This study will utilize a combination of descriptive and developmental research design. The developmental component will be used to design, develop, test, and evaluate BUDGIE, a personal financial management app for Filipinos, by translating the requirements identified for the intended users into working system features. The descriptive component will supply these requirements by examining the existing financial management practices of the intended users and identifying the problems they encounter in budgeting, saving, and managing debt, with its findings serving as the basis for the developmental component.

The developmental component will focus on the design, development, testing, and evaluation of BUDGIE based on the requirements identified during the descriptive phase. The reference expense data and the seasonal expense patterns established during the descriptive phase will serve as inputs to the system’s analytical models. The development process will translate the identified requirements into system features built around the four models of the study: rule-based profile classification, SARIMA-based seasonal expense forecasting, IQR-based anomaly detection for unusual expenses, and linear programming for personalized budgets, per-goal savings contribution schedules, and per-debt repayment schedules under financial constraints. These models will be integrated into application features for expense recording, budgeting, savings and debt planning, seasonal spending forecasts, and unusual spending alerts, culminating in an actionable financial plan presented to the user for their approval. The completed system will undergo testing to verify functionality and reliability, followed by evaluation using structured instruments, including the System Usability Scale and measures aligned with the ISO/IEC 25010 software quality model. Testing and evaluation will be conducted against the requirements established in the descriptive phase.

The descriptive component will be used to examine the financial management situation of Filipinos aged 18–59 in the National Capital Region, with the requirements of the intended users determined on the basis of the Public User Expectations and Perception Survey (PUEPS). The existing budgeting, saving, and debt management practices of the intended users, together with the spending characteristics and patterns observed in the study’s reference expense data, will be organized and analyzed using descriptive statistical techniques. The output of this phase is a set of user and system requirements, which then serve as the basis for the design and development of the proposed application.
